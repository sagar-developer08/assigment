PROSPECT / FIELD NOTES 01

The Client
Launch Playbook.

A sample resource for the paid-access exercise.

01 · Define the outcome

Write down who your project serves, the problem it solves, and the evidence that will demonstrate success. Agree on the smallest useful release.

02 · Build a reliable foundation

Keep identity, payment, access, and integration status separate. Validate trust boundaries, use stable identifiers, and record meaningful events.

03 · Plan for the imperfect day

Assume requests will repeat and services will fail. Make operations safe to retry and preserve customer access when secondary systems are unavailable.

04 · Launch with clarity

Test the complete journey. Document setup, unfinished work, and operational recovery. Demonstrate behavior with a real test purchase.
